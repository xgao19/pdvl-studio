# PDVL engineering instructions
Keep PDVL provider-agnostic and research-state-first. Do not store secrets in git.
Do not promote AI statements to verified physics. Verify with independent, scoped tests.
Keep Discuss and Write natural and physically incisive; no default engineering-report prose.
Never submit HPC jobs or mutate external research datasets without explicit approval.
See `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` before changing the model gateway or evidence schema.
